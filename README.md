# LeukoScan
An AI-Driven Image SegmentationAnd Classification System for Early Diagnosis of Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia

ABSTRACT

Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (ALL) is a hematological malignancy that is rapidly progressive among children and young adults and exact and early diagnosis is a major factor to enhance patient survival. Traditional diagnostic services are based on the manual microscopic examination of peripheral blood smears by qualified pathologists. Even though effective, it is a labor-intensive, subjective, and inter-observer variant process, which may easily lead to inter-observer variation, especially in resource-constrained clinical settings. We present LeukoScan, a two-stage deep learning system, in this paper to automate the process of detecting and classifying ALL in images of peripheral blood smear in this paper. The system proposed incorporates two supplementary modules. In phase one, a convolutional neural network with U-Net is used to semantically segment leukemic blast cells in full-slide smear images, obtaining a Dice Similarity Coefficient of 85.96% and an Intersection over Union of 76.56% on the test data. The second stage involves a ResNet-50 model with transfer learning to classify single cells as normal or malignant with a classification accuracy of 92.31. Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM), which gives visual explanations of the model in terms of highlighting diagnostically interesting nuclear and chromatin events, is added to improve the clinical interpretability of the model. The system is tested on the ALL-IDB data, and it has a high sensitivity, and this is required to reduce false-negative results in the screening of leukemia. In general, LeukoScan is a fast, objective, and explainable diagnostic support tool, which can potentially benefit clinicians, especially in the practice environment where the use of professional hematopathologists is limited.
